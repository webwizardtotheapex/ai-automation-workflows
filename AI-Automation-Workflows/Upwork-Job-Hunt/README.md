# 🚀 Upwork Job Hunt with Telegram Alert

An AI-powered job monitoring workflow built with **n8n** that automatically searches for new Upwork jobs, filters relevant opportunities, and instantly delivers notifications to Telegram so you never miss a potential client.

---

## 📌 Overview

Searching Upwork manually every few minutes is time-consuming and can lead to missed opportunities.

This workflow automates the entire process by continuously monitoring Upwork job listings based on predefined search criteria and sending instant Telegram notifications whenever a matching job is found.

The automation helps freelancers and agencies respond quickly to new job postings, increasing the chances of winning projects.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔍 Automatically searches Upwork for new jobs
- 🤖 Filters jobs based on custom keywords
- 📨 Sends instant Telegram notifications
- ⚡ Eliminates manual job searching
- 📱 Mobile-friendly alerts
- 🔄 Fully automated workflow

---
